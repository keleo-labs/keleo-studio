Project Planning Report Structure
To effectively adopt a methodology for project planning, a practitioner needs to understand the "Why" (Strategic Intent), the "When" (Lifecycle Phases), the "What" (Milestones and Deliverables), and the "Who/How" (Teams and Activities).

1. Strategic Context & Business Case: Extracts the root Method definition and its narratives to help the project planner understand the ultimate goal and business justification.

2. Project Lifecycle & Phasing: Extracts the patterns and patternViews to define the chronological macro-phases (Sprints or Release Trains) of the project.

3. Milestones & Deliverables: Extracts alphas (state-driven goals) and workProducts (evidentiary artifacts). This forms the Definition of Done (DoD) and the artifact backlog.

4. Resourcing & Activity Backlog: Extracts personaGroups, personas, and activities to define the cross-functional teams required and the specific operational workflows they must execute.
