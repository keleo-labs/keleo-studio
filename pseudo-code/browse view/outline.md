# Methodology Documentation Structure
A highly effective rendering of this schema follows a five-part structure:

1. Executive Context: The overall method identity and its root narratives.

2. Lifecycle Orchestration (Patterns): The temporal phases of the methodology, providing the practitioner with a chronological roadmap.

3. Core Concepts & Progression (Alphas): The abstract areas of concern and their sequential states of maturity.

4. Evidentiary Artifacts (Work Products): The physical deliverables that prove the maturation of the core concepts.

5. Execution & Roles (Activities & Personas): The specific workflows, who executes them, and the competencies required.
